The terrain textures, 48-colour palette, cuboid character/prop models and rigid animations are original project assets. Editable Blockbench sources, including animation keyframes, are under `assets/source/models/`; procedural source is `tools/make_models.py`. No external art pack is used.

The 16-pixel UI panel, focus, button, checkbox, slider and arrow textures are original palette-only artwork generated by `tools/make_ui.py`. The shared nine-slice theme and original PNGs are under `game/assets/ui/`. Silkscreen is the separately licensed, pinned upstream font listed above.

The sound effects, synthesized fictional vocal cues, ambience and 144-second **Lanterns under the Hollow** music score are original procedural project assets. Editable score/presets and provenance hashes are under `assets/source/audio/`; the generator is `tools/make_audio.py`. No sampled recordings or external music are used. Listening/device review remains a separate acceptance gate.

The complete Godot 4.4.1 and .NET runtime 8.0.32 licence/third-party texts are preserved under `game/licenses/` and included in the PCK/APK by the export filter. The local Android probe uses Eclipse Temurin JDK 17; it is build tooling, not exported game content. Its distribution includes its own licence and notices under `.toolchain/jdk17/`.

Before M8 distribution, audit this bundle against the final engine/runtime/font and any new assets, and add the required attribution screen/package notices.
